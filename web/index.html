What Prism checks

Text contrast WCAG 1.4.3
Text against its background, at least 4.5 : 1, or 3 : 1 for large text.
Target size WCAG 2.5.8
Interactive targets at least 24 × 24 CSS px.
Visible label WCAG 3.3.2
Form fields with a visible label, not only placeholder text.
